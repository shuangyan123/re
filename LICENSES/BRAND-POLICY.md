# TutorBench Brand Policy

This is a project policy for the TutorBench name and visual brand assets. It
is separate from the Apache-2.0 software license and the CC BY 4.0 benchmark
content license. Those licenses do not grant unrestricted permission to use
TutorBench names, logos, visual assets, or an official identity.

This policy is written as a practical project boundary. It does not make a
claim that a TutorBench name or asset is a registered trademark, does not
state a trademark registration number, and does not identify a legal entity
or legal representative.

## Scope

The policy covers the TutorBench name, the `assets/brand/tutorbench/` visual
assets, the approved T1 marks, and presentations that could make a third
party appear to be the official TutorBench project.

## Allowed referential use

You may make accurate, non-confusing nominative or referential use of the
name, including:

- “Evaluated with TutorBench.”
- “Compatible with TutorBench.”
- “Derived from TutorBench.”
- “A third-party experiment based on TutorBench.”
- Identifying TutorBench in an academic paper, presentation, article, video,
  demo, or screenshot.
- Linking to the official repository or official project website.

Use the name or an unmodified official logo only as needed to identify the
project. A reference must not imply that the TutorBench maintainers reviewed,
certified, ranked, sponsored, or endorsed the third-party work.

## Uses that are not permitted without explicit maintainer permission

Do not:

- Name a fork or derived benchmark “TutorBench Official,” “TutorBench China,”
  or “TutorBench Pro.”
- Call a third-party ranking page an “Official TutorBench Leaderboard.”
- Use a TutorBench logo as the primary brand or app icon for another product,
  service, plugin, or application.
- Modify, recolor, stretch, crop, or combine a TutorBench logo in a way that
  suggests official project identity, then continue to present it as an
  official mark.
- Suggest that TutorBench maintainers certify third-party results, rankings,
  products, integrations, or services.
- Use visual presentation, naming, or placement that is likely to confuse
  users about origin, sponsorship, or endorsement.

“Derived from TutorBench” is an accurate source reference when true. It must
not be presented as “Official TutorBench.”

## Brand contributions

Ordinary code, case, documentation, or issue contributions do not authorize
replacement, alteration, or relicensing of TutorBench brand assets. Propose a
brand change separately and preserve the approved asset provenance.

Questions about a proposed use can be raised through the repository’s normal
GitHub maintainer route without including sensitive information. A request
is not approval until a maintainer explicitly grants it.
